## Knowledge boundaries

### Author expansion of Viator history

The author's latest account supersedes the source claim that the Viators were born from the Weave: their origin is unknown. It establishes their peaceful nature, equal consideration for creatures and mountains, exceptional wisdom and precision, centuries of resentment among dissatisfied pupils, and the initially unrecognized irreversible cost of forced magic. Shovresk refined the technique in hiding over years, surpassed most Viators in power, and fought in some of the smaller clashes preceding the final battle. The Viators underestimated the destructive method's potential. The final release of power is comparable to direct exposure to the Weave in the Astral Plane.

Naming remains provisional: the author's expansion uses both Val Kaarun and Val Shovresk for the magician. The manuscript retains Val Shovresk for continuity and Val'kaárun for the battle pending clarification. No second magician has been invented.

### Withdrawn chapter and world foundations

Author's cosmology clarification: use "plane of existence" as the primary term, with "dimension" as a synonym. The Astral Plane surrounds and contains the other planes and the Weave; the Weave permeates and surrounds everything within it. Omit the cosmic Vortex from this presentation, superseding the older source hierarchy described below. Anoxis is the closest plane to Eides, making convergence easier. These are direct author clarifications, not deductions from the German notes. Retain "dimensional convergence" as the established name of the phenomenon.

The author has authorized a substantial account of the world, Weave, Viators, their origins and departure, pantheon, and planes for real readers. The Viator chapter is marked withdrawn from the circulated in-world volume. This replaces the earlier decision to leave that history entirely undrafted. Rare knowledge can appear here without becoming publicly available in the setting. The reason for withdrawal, preservation of the chapter, and the compiler's exceptional access remain open; no censorship incident or secret source has been invented.

The world and pantheon foundation is separate from the withdrawn chapter. No exact boundary between public and restricted cosmological knowledge is yet established. The explanatory note addressing the reader is editorial framing, not a claim made by the original chronicler.
