# Chronicles

An in-world historical work compiled by a principal historian and his scholars in the Arch-God Kingdom of Man during the period of the Dark Storms.

The main volumes present the school's established understanding at the time of compilation. Earlier history may be fragmentary or uncertain; events closer to the compilers' own time can receive fuller treatment. Dates and subjects need not be exhaustively known before a volume is written.

Each volume can contain a chronological account and longer narratives of particular events, places, and people. These are editorial periods for this project; they do not replace the wiki's historical era definitions.

## Additional volumes within each period

Later investigations and stories are published as additional volumes belonging to the same historical period. The original volume remains Volume I; its first additional collection is Volume I.II, followed by Volume I.III, and so on. Early History follows the same pattern: Volume II, Volume II.II, Volume II.III.

Keep these additional volumes inside the corresponding period folder, rather than in a separate collection. For example, Ancient History contains the original manuscript in `volume-I.md` and later collections in `volume-I.II.md`, `volume-I.III.md`, and so on. These are separate publications, not revisions or required intermediate steps in the main reading order.

Additional volumes may introduce previously unrecorded kingdoms, cities, people, or events, or give fuller treatment to known subjects. Identify each account's historical period near the beginning; approximate or unknown dates are acceptable. Where useful, explain how the subject came to the school's attention. Keep an account together even when it spans several periods, placing it in the folder of its principal subject.

Published main volumes remain intact. Each additional account can be read as a self-contained narrative, and later collections can be arranged by subject or compilation order without requiring a complete chronology in advance.

Future volumes or continuations should reflect the knowledge and authorship available at their in-world compilation date.
